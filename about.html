About Fresh Bakes Bakery

Fresh Bakes Bakery is a small local business that started in 2022 in Johannesburg, South Africa. It was started by a baker named Sarah Molefe, who wanted to offer customers freshly baked muffins, bread, cakes and pastries at reasonbale prices. She began making her goods at home and selling them to her friends, neighbors, family and local businesses. As more people showed interest, she decided to open a small bakery and make her freshly baked products available to more customrs.

The bakery offers a variety of products suitable for everyday enjoyment as well as birthdays, celebrations and other special occasions. Customers can choose from different types of bread, caks,cupcakes,muffins and pastries. Each product is prepared with care and attention to quality, presentation and freshness. Fresh Bakes Bakery aims to provide customers with baked goods that are enjoyable ro share with family, friends and other people on special occasions.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide customers with fresh, delicious and carefully prepared baked products while creating a warm and welcoming bakery experience. Fresh Bakes Bakery aims to deliver friendly customers service and provide products at fair and reasonable prices.

We also aim to build positive relationships with our customers by listening to their needs, responding to enquiries and continously improving the quality and presentation of our products. Through our website and social media platforms, we want customers to easily discover our products, contact the bakery and stay connected with Fresh Bakes Bakery.

Our vision

Our vision is to become a trusted local bakery known for quality products, excellent service and a welcoming customer experience. We aim to build lasting relationships with our customers by consistently providing fresh, delicious and well-presented baked goods.
